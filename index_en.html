Welcome to Karlskrona December Swing 2026!

Karlskrona December Swing takes place on 4-6 December and is filled to the brim with Lindy hop. Classes are held on Saturday and Sunday, with social dance to live music on Friday and Saturday evening.

Friday Evening

Social dance in Filmsalen, Gullberna Park, Karlskrona, to Arnö Revival Stompers and Swing DJ.

Taster: Intro to Lindy hop for beginners 7:00 PM - 8:00 PM. For you who hasn't previously tried Lindy hop, wants to try the "other" role, or just wants to warm up before the dance.

Dance! 8:00 PM - 11:30 PM. Registration from 6:30 PM.

Saturday evening

Social dance in Filmsalen, Gullberna Park, Karlskrona, to Underground Swing and Swing DJ.

Taster: 7:00 PM - 8:00 PM (To Be Determined)

Dance! 8:00 PM - 11:30 PM

Workshop Saturday and Sunday

One level; Lindy hop (at least one year's experience recommended)

Registration on Saturday 9:30 AM - 10:00 AM, at the entrance to Filmsalen, Gullberna Park, Karlskrona.

Saturday: The classes take place at Filmsalen from 10:00 AM. The last class is expected to end before 4:00 PM. Lunch at around 12:00 PM - 1:15 PM.

Sundag: The classes take place at Filmsalen from 10:00 AM. The last class is expected to end before 4:00 PM. Lunch at around 12:00 PM - 1:15 PM.

Teachers: Charlie and Rebecka Decavita

Price

Workshop + social dance: 1100 SEK (regular price) / 800 SEK (student/retiree)

Social dance, per evening: 150 SEK (regular price) / 100 SEK (student/retiree)

Write that you're a student/retiree when applying, and don't forget to bring your student ID to the registration!

Karlskrona December Swing is organised by Tipsy Dance Club with support from Karlskrona Kommun, Musik i Blekinge and Sisu-Idrottsutbildarna, Blekinge
