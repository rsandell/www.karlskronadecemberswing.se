With more than 20 years of experience of dancing and teaching together in more than 25 countries, The DecaVitas has made Lindy Hop their common language.

Their teaching builds on connection, musicality, and a relaxed, effective leader and follower technique that works equally well in both higher tempos as well as in slower dance. The focus is in the collaboration between two dancers and what happens when both get the space to add to, listen to, and play with the music.

Because when the connection is flawless one and one becomes three. And good technique should not just look good, it should also feel good and last the entire night.
